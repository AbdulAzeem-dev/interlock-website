# INTERLOCK — Content Strategy
Internal planning document. Not published on the website.

## Voice rules
- No banned phrases: "where quality meets innovation", "we bring your vision to
  life", "your trusted partner for all your needs", "excellence in every stitch",
  "transforming ideas into reality", "unmatched quality and service", "at INTERLOCK
  we believe", "welcome to INTERLOCK".
- No unsupported superlatives: world-class, revolutionary, cutting-edge, best,
  leading, unmatched, unparalleled, luxury, premium, excellence — used only if a
  specific fact backs the claim, and even then sparingly.
- Prefer concrete process statements over adjectives. "Share a reference garment or
  specification; we review construction, materials, sizing, and branding" beats
  "premium quality guaranteed."
- Zero em-dashes anywhere (headlines, body, captions, quotes) per the design skill's
  hard ban — hyphens or restructured sentences only.
- British/neutral international English, consistent spelling.

## Primary CTA labels (locked, used everywhere — no duplicate-intent CTAs)
- Primary: "Discuss Your Requirements"
- Secondary: "Explore Our Capabilities"
- Form submission: "Start an Enquiry" / "Send Enquiry"
Never "Shop Now", never "Get Started" (SaaS-coded), never mix "Contact Us" and
"Get in Touch" and "Talk to Us" on the same page.

## Headline directions (as shipped)
- Hero: "Knitwear manufacturing, refined over four decades."
- Product range: "One manufacturing partner. A wide knitwear range."
- Manufacturer proof: "Proof is in the process."
- Heritage: "Manufacturing knitwear since 1982."
- Private label: "Start with your product brief."

## Handling missing facts honestly
Where a number or fact is not confirmed (capacity, machine count, staff count,
certifications, lead times, MOQ), copy uses qualitative, honest language instead of
inventing a figure — e.g. "production runs are planned around your delivery window"
rather than a fake "14-day lead time." Every such substitution is logged in
REQUIRED_BUSINESS_INPUTS.md with the exact question the owner needs to answer and
where the real figure would slot in.

## Copy self-audit
Every visible string is re-read before shipping for: grammatical correctness, no
AI-hallucination artefacts, no forced wordplay, one register per page (no mixing
technical spec-sheet tone with marketing punch inside the same section).
