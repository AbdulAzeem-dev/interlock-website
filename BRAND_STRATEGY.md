# WINTEX — Brand Strategy
Internal planning document. Not published on the website.

## What WINTEX is
A Pakistan-based knitwear manufacturer established in 1982. Forty-four years of
production experience across sweaters, cardigans, uniform knitwear, knitted cotton
shirts, and knitted accessories (socks, gloves, mufflers, scarves, caps). Historically
strong in the Pakistani school and college uniform market, with additional experience
manufacturing specification-led military-style knitwear through a Pakistani export
partner (third-party manufacturer, not a direct government/military supplier).
Now building direct B2B relationships with international brands, distributors,
institutional buyers, and sourcing teams.

## What WINTEX is not
- Not a retail brand, not e-commerce, no cart, no prices.
- Not only a school-uniform manufacturer.
- Not only a military-knitwear manufacturer.
- Not a trading company — a factory with decades of production history.

## Positioning statement
A trusted Pakistani knitwear manufacturer established in 1982, producing custom
knitwear for international brands, distributors, institutions, and sourcing partners.

Supporting line: "Four decades of knitwear manufacturing, built around your
specifications."

## Why international expansion (public framing)
Frame as a natural next stage after decades of domestic manufacturing experience —
bringing established production knowledge to direct global partnerships. Never
reference local payment delays, market pressure, or financial motives. This
framing lives in About and the international-buyer CTA copy.

## Audiences and what each cares about
1. Fashion / private-label brands — design interpretation, yarns, sampling, labels,
   packaging, consistency, communication.
2. School/college uniform distributors — durability, colour and sizing consistency,
   logo/embroidery accuracy, seasonal delivery, long-term supply.
3. Military/technical-product contractors — specification control, documentation,
   consistency, confidentiality, repeat production.
4. Wholesalers/distributors — range, reliability, lead times, quantity capability,
   repeat ordering.
5. Institutional/corporate-uniform suppliers — specification compliance, repeatability.

No single audience should visually dominate the homepage. Product range section
gives equal visual weight to fashion, uniform, institutional, and accessory
categories.

## Tone
Calm, precise, confident, evidence-led. No superlatives without support ("world-class",
"leading", "unmatched" are banned). Explain process instead of asserting quality.
British/neutral international English throughout.

## Non-negotiable facts
- Brand name: WINTEX
- Established: 1982 (never 1988, never any other year)
- Location: Pakistan (stated confidently, never hidden)
- Descriptor: "Fine Knitwear Manufacturers"

## Unverified claims policy
Anything not confirmed by the owner (capacity, machine count, employee count,
certifications, named clients, export countries, lead times, MOQs) is excluded from
the site and logged in REQUIRED_BUSINESS_INPUTS.md with honest placeholder language
used instead. No fabricated statistics, logos, or certificates anywhere.
